1. Group 1 – Emotional Companionship Seekers
Persona: Lê Gia Hân, 21
A third-year university student living in District 7. She enjoys Wattpad stories and romance novels, especially characters who are emotionally mature, warm, understanding, and attentive—such as a caring older sibling, a thoughtful partner, or a gentle best friend.
A. Customer Segment – Customer Profile
Age: Typically 18–25.
Profile: Young people seeking emotional connection, companionship, and a space to express personal feelings.
Interests: Romance novels, Wattpad, films, audio stories, and conversations with fictional characters.
Behaviors: Often use the app at night or during stressful moments; tend to return to the same AI character over time.
Willingness to pay: May pay for personalized experiences when the character feels consistent, remembers them, and provides meaningful interaction.
B. Customer Pain Points
Feel uncomfortable sharing private matters with friends or family for fear of being judged.
General-purpose chatbots can feel generic and emotionally shallow.
AI characters may drift away from their original personality, tone, or behavior.
The AI may forget personal details, previous conversations, and meaningful shared experiences.
Users want a personalized, ongoing interaction rather than repeatedly starting from zero.

Core Job
When I feel lonely, stressed, or emotionally overwhelmed, I want to create and interact with an AI character who understands me and remembers our shared experiences, so that I can express myself freely and feel emotionally supported.
Functional Jobs
Create an AI character with a preferred personality, appearance, tone of voice, and behavior.
Define the relationship with the character, such as a best friend, partner, older sibling, or mentor.
Build a personal setting and story around the relationship.
Enable the AI to remember personal information, conversations, and important shared moments.
Maintain consistent personality and responses across multiple sessions.
Emotional Jobs
Feel heard, understood, and acknowledged.
Have a private-feeling space to share without fear of judgment.
Experience familiarity and a sense of continuity over time.
Social Jobs
Explore preferred relationship dynamics and communication styles.
Express personal identity and interests through a self-created AI character.
D. Product Value
The app lets users create an AI companion tailored to their preferences—from personality and relationship dynamics to the surrounding story. With memory and continuity, the character can build on previous conversations and shared experiences, creating a more personal and consistent companionship experience.
2. Group 2 – Creative Roleplay & Fiction Enthusiasts
Persona: Phạm Quốc Bảo, 20
A second-year university student who enjoys anime, manga, and light novels. He follows fandom communities, creates original characters (OCs), and enjoys roleplay, world-building, and developing stories with multiple characters and plotlines.
A. Customer Segment – Customer Profile
Age: Typically 16–28.
Profile: Creative users who enjoy fictional worlds, interactive storytelling, and roleplay.
Interests: Anime, manga, role-playing games (RPGs), fantasy, romance, science fiction, fanfiction, and world-building.
Behaviors: Frequently create characters, develop fictional settings, roleplay, and participate in fandom communities; may spend extended periods developing a story.
Willingness to pay: May pay for advanced features such as expanded character creation, long-term memory, world customization, and deeper roleplay experiences.
B. Customer Pain Points
Existing platforms may limit how deeply users can customize characters and settings.
It can be difficult to find roleplay partners with compatible interests, styles, and storytelling ability.
AI may break immersion by forgetting prior events, changing character traits, or contradicting established facts.
Long stories involving multiple characters, relationships, and complex plotlines are difficult to maintain.
Some platforms lack integrated tools for organizing worlds, characters, relationships, and story developments.
Language barriers and international pricing can make some apps less accessible.

World-building: Set the world, era, locations, rules, cultures, and social systems.

Roleplay Interaction: Roleplay directly with AI and influence how the story unfolds.
Long-term Memory: Preserve important events, character details, relationships, and previous choices.
Story Control: Edit, redirect, or expand the story as creative ideas evolve.

Feel free to create and express imagination.
Become immersed in a world of their own making.
Enjoy discovering unexpected developments as the story unfolds.
Feel a sense of ownership and control over their fictional world and characters.
Social Jobs

Share characters or fictional worlds with like-minded communities.
Connect with fandom, creative-writing, and roleplay communities.
